Cinelace

Video editor for travel footage.

Cinelace analyses your clips, suggests moments, and builds a sequence you can edit. Processing runs on your computer, from analysis to the final export.

Contact the developerYouTube channel

Workflow

  1. Import and analyse

    Add your travel videos. Cinelace checks shots, sharpness, exposure, motion, and speech to identify candidate moments.

  2. Generate a sequence

    Choose a chronological Travel Story or a Highlight Montage. Set the target length, review the sequence, and preview the selected clips.

  3. Edit and export

    Reorder, trim, or replace moments. Add music, adjust speech and music levels, and export the result as an MP4 video.

Features in development

Clip analysis
Shot detection and quality, motion, and speech analysis.
Sequence editing
Reorder, trim, lock clips, and select alternative moments.
Audio
Music playlists, volume controls, and speech ducking.
Export
1080p or 4K MP4 output.
Local processing
Analysis and editing run on your computer. Footage is not uploaded for processing.
