Cinelace

Automatically create a video, then make it your own.

Cinelace uses open AI models to turn your footage into a finished video automatically. If you want to make changes, you can refine the result on the editor timeline.

Contact the developerYouTube channel

Workflow

  1. Add your footage

    Choose the clips you want to include.

  2. Create automatically

    Cinelace uses open AI models to analyse the footage and assemble a video.

  3. Make it yours

    Use the editor timeline to adjust the result if you want, then export your video.
